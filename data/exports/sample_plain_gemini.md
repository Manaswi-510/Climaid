### 1. Ward Overview in Plain Words
In Ward #19 (Vadgaon Sheri - Ganesh Nagar), life gets very difficult during extreme weather because basic civic setups like roadside drains and trees are almost completely missing. In the summer, the lack of shade and open green spaces turns streets and tin-roof homes into heat traps with temperatures crossing 34°C. During the monsoon, even light rain quickly pools on the roads and around slum pockets because 98% of the streets have nowhere for the water to drain away.

---

### 2. Ward Climate Problems & Practical Solutions

| Climate Problem | How Severe? | Why It Happens in This Ward | Simple Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flooding & Waterlogging** | Very High | 98.2% of roads have no drains at all, so rainwater has nowhere to go. | Clear out existing choked drains and put up metal trash guards to let water flow quickly. |
| **Extreme Heat** | High | Tin roofs in slum areas soak up heat, and there are no shaded bus shelters or free water spots. | Paint roofs with heat-reflecting white coating and set up a shaded bus stop with drinking water. |
| **Zero Greenery** | High | There are zero officially recorded trees or public parks, leaving roads bare and hot. | Plant native shade trees along 1 kilometer of main walking roads. |
| **Dirty Water / Sanitation** | High | 80% of slum homes are not linked to sewage pipes, causing dirty water to mix with rainwater. | Keep main water channels clean and trash-free so runoff doesn't back up into homes. |

### 4. Step-by-Step Practical Ground Plan

1. **Week 1 – Drain Desilting Kickoff:** The ward engineer brings in workers to dig out mud and trash from the critical bottleneck drain and fit iron mesh screens so garbage cannot block the flow again before rains start.
2. **Week 2 to 4 – Cool Roof Painting:** Team up with local community leaders to paint roofs in the two selected slum clusters with sun-reflecting white paint before peak heat days.
3. **Month 1 to 2 – Build Bus Shelter & Water Kiosk:** Coordinate with PMPML to set up a shaded shelter with a clean, working drinking water tap at the busiest local bus stop.
4. **Month 2 to 3 – Sapling Plantation & Tree Guards:** The Garden Department digs pits and plants strong local shade trees (like Neem and Peepal) along 1 km of roadside, securing them with tree guards.

### 5. How Will We Know It Worked? (Simple Checklist)

- [ ] **No Standing Water:** After a downpour, rainwater clears off the main roads within 30 to 45 minutes instead of staying logged for hours.
- [ ] **Noticeably Cooler Homes:** Residents under painted roofs report their rooms feel much cooler and bearable in the hot afternoon.
- [ ] **Working Street Shade & Water:** Daily commuters and pedestrians are actively using the new shaded bus stand, drinking the free water, and newly planted saplings are surviving with guards intact.
